Side, top, and bottom views all have a crop control. At 0% the whole circle is kept with a finite projection, and you set the aspect ratio.

How much of the circle is removed at the top and at the bottom. 0% means no crop.

Used only at 0% crop. 1 is square, 2 is twice as tall. Default 1.58.

Where to start unwrapping the circle. 0° is the right side. A full turn returns to the same picture.

Center 0% is the middle of the photo. Drag the cross on the original to move it. Radius 100% touches the shorter side. Match the gold ring to the fisheye edge. Moving the center does not change the radius.

Keep only part of the result. Drag the gold box, pull a corner or edge, or use the sliders. Save JPEG keeps the box.
